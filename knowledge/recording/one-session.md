# One recording session (30–60 minutes)

English only. Phone or better camera on a stable support, lav if available, front light, plain wall.

## Summary

Record consent, expressive motion, a ~2 minute monologue, framing stills, and 15–30 minutes of calm English voice. The twin says the final script later — this session is source material.

## Gotchas

1. Thin 15-second-only takes fail twin quality → also shoot a continuous ~2 minute monologue.
2. Wide smile stills warp identity → lips slightly open, subtle expression.
3. Nervous voice samples become the ad delivery → record calm, slightly slow English for the clone.
4. Huge logos on clothing become permanent looks → wear what the ad should live in.
